# What iSpeakLLM does not promise

iSpeakLLM makes a site's content easy for AI systems to read, states the site's terms in every machine-readable form
that exists today, and records which agents fetched what. That is all it claims.

- **No traffic, ranking or citation promises.** Serving Markdown or llms.txt does not make any AI product show,
  cite or link a site more often. As of November 2026, no major AI provider's crawler documentation says its bots read llms.txt.
- **Preferences are not enforcement.** Content-Signal, AIPREF, robots.txt, RSL, ai.txt and TDM reservation express
  terms. They block nothing. A crawler that ignores them is not stopped by them.
- **A User-Agent is a claim.** Only a verified Web Bot Auth signature names an operator. Everything else is reported
  as claimed or unverified, never as fact.
- **A signed manifest proves who said what, and when.** It does not prove that the statements in it are true or
  lawful; that stays the site operator's responsibility.
- **Not legal advice.** The declaration helps express a TDM reservation or AI-use terms; whether they are effective
  in a given jurisdiction is a legal question.
